Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024

Introduction

Dandy ("we", "us", "our") operates the Dandy mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

No Data Collection

Dandy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No photos are uploaded to our servers
  • No usage analytics are collected
  • No personal information is tracked
  • No third-party trackers are integrated
  • No internet connection is required (or permitted)

How Dandy Works

All photo analysis happens exclusively on your device:

  • Image recognition runs on-device using bundled machine learning models
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device
  • Descriptions and indexes are stored only in your phone's private app storage
  • Location data is read from your photo EXIF tags (if present) and stored locally

Permissions We Request

Dandy requests the following permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: To read your photos
  • ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: To read GPS data embedded in photos (optional)

These are purely read-only and used only to analyze photos on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

What We Explicitly Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • We do not use advertising networks
  • We do not track your behavior
  • We do not require sign-in or accounts
  • We do not backup your data to cloud servers

Internet Permission

Dandy does not request INTERNET permission. The app cannot send or receive data from the network, even if it wanted to. This is enforced at the system level by the Android manifest.

Opt-In Features

Some features are optional:

  • Save descriptions in photos: You can choose to write descriptions into your photo files' EXIF metadata. This is done locally and requires your explicit permission per batch.
  • Location tagging: You can grant or revoke location access at any time in your phone settings.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data Dandy has created by clearing its storage in your phone settings, or by using the "Forget everything" button in the app. This removes:

  • All descriptions
  • All photo indexes
  • All cached information

Your original photos are never touched.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@dandy.app
