Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

This policy explains what the Dandy Android app does with information. Short version: Dandy does not collect, store on any server, or transmit your data. Everything happens on your phone.

What Dandy processes

To organise your library, Dandy reads the following on your device:

  • Your photos, read-only, through Android's media permissions.
  • Location stored inside a photo's file (if present), through ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION, to name places. Optional.
  • The words visible in photos, image labels, face positions, and a numeric "meaning" vector per photo. Computed by models bundled in the app.

These results are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, in files named index.json and photo_vectors.bin.

What Dandy does not do

  • It does not request the INTERNET or network-state permissions. Android blocks network access for the app.
  • It sends nothing to us or to anyone else. We run no servers that receive app data.
  • It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
  • It does not change your original photos. Optional "save into photo" writes a text description into the file's metadata, only after you confirm, and you can remove it.

Permissions

  • Photo access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android): to read your library.
  • Visual user selected (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): if you choose to share only some photos.
  • Media location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): to read location tags in photos. Can be refused.

Deleting your data

Use Forget everything in Settings to delete all indexes and vectors Dandy has created. Uninstalling the app removes the rest. Your photos are never affected.

Children

Dandy is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
