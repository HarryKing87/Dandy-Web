What Dandy does

🏷️

Automatic Sections

Dandy groups photos into Selfies, People, Pets, Food, Places, and more. No tagging by hand.

🔍

Search by Meaning

Type "coffee on a table" or "sunset at beach" and find exactly what you're looking for.

🗺️

Places Map

See where your photos were taken, right on your phone's offline map.

🔒

100% Private

No network permission, no uploads, no servers. Everything stays on your device.

⚡

Runs on Battery

Dandy only works when charging. Battery, location, and privacy fully under your control.

📝

Optional File Save

Write descriptions into your photo files so they travel with you to other apps.

Privacy First

Your photos, your phone

Dandy uses on-device machine learning to understand your photos. No photos leave your device. No servers, no cloud, no tracking.

We built Dandy because we wanted to organize our own photos without giving them to anyone else. That's still how it works.

  • Photo analysis runs on your phone
  • Descriptions are written locally
  • No internet permission at all
  • No ads, no analytics, no sign-in
