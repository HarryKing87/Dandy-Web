What it does

Built for the photos you actually have.

Screenshots, receipts, camera rolls. Dandy handles all of them the same way.

Reads the words in a picture

A screenshot of a 475-day streak can be found by searching 475. Signs, receipts, menus and chats are searchable too.

Search by meaning

Type a sentence the way you remember it. Dandy compares it with what each photo shows.

Sections, not folders

Selfies, people, pets, food, cars, documents, screenshots. Each appears only if it has photos.

Places on a map

Photos with location data are grouped by town. The map works offline too.

Open in your gallery, any time

Dandy never changes your originals. Tap a photo and open it in the app you already use. Descriptions can optionally be saved into the photo file, and removed again.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the phone.

  • 01

    No internet permission

    The app manifest removes INTERNET, so Android refuses any network access.

  • 02

    On-device models only

    Image labels, faces, text and meaning vectors are all computed locally from models bundled in the app.

  • 03

    No accounts, no analytics

    There is nothing to sign in to and nothing reporting back.

  • 04

    Forget everything

    One button clears every index and vector Dandy has made. Your photos are never touched.

How it works

Three steps, then it stays out of the way.

  1. 1

    Allow photo access

    Choose all photos, or only the ones you select.

  2. 2

    Arrange

    Dandy reads the library in the background, by default only while the phone charges.

  3. 3

    Look around

    Browse sections, search, or see your places. Search unlocks once the first pass finishes.
